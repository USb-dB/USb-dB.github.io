I am an undergraduate student in Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, where I am also pursuing a minor in Nanotechnology. My academic work focuses on integrated photonics, quantum photonics, and photonic computing, especially the design of scalable photonic circuits for quantum information processing.

I am currently working on integrated quantum photonics and programmable silicon photonic circuits. My projects span spatial-mode switching with Mach-Zehnder interferometer meshes, frequency-bin cluster-state generation, and FPGA-controlled memory interfaces. Across these areas, I am interested in how circuit design, control, and realistic device imperfections shape the performance of photonic and electronic systems.

Alongside research, I have worked on projects at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, IIT Kharagpur, and IIT Bombay. I have also served as Co-Coordinator of the Electronics and IoT Club at IIT (ISM), helping organize hands-on workshops and technical activities for students.

## Research interests

- Integrated quantum photonics

- Programmable photonic circuits
- Photonic computing and quantum information processing

## Current work

My current research includes designing and simulating integrated components such as multimode-interference (MMI) couplers, directional couplers, and dual-mode Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) switches. I have designed Clements MZI meshes for dual-mode and higher-order spatial-mode switching, and carried out GDS layout and post-layout analysis in KLayout. I am also investigating Gaussian Boson Sampling architectures using programmable meshes.

In frequency-bin photonics, I have worked on programmable n-rail cluster-state circuits, using Strawberry Fields to generate cluster states from EPR states and the van Loock-Furusawa criteria to assess entanglement. My analysis also considers how fabrication-induced loss and power imbalance affect noise and nullifier variances.

My earlier hardware work includes a Verilog-based controller for an 8x8 RRAM crossbar on a DE0-Nano FPGA. That project combined digital read/write sequencing with analog sensing, simulation, timing analysis, and experimental testing.
