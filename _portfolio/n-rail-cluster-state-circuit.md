At FOS Lab, IIT Kharagpur, I worked on an integrated photonic circuit for programmable continuous-variable cluster states encoded across frequency bins. The circuit was designed to generate n-rail cluster-state structures from entangled EPR inputs, providing a route to reconfigurable photonic resources for quantum information processing.

I used Strawberry Fields to generate and study 0D-3D cluster states, then evaluated their entanglement using the van Loock-Furusawa (VLF) criteria. I also analysed how fabrication-related loss and power imbalance affect cluster-state noise and nullifier variances, connecting ideal circuit models with practical implementation constraints.

<strong>Tools and methods:</strong> Strawberry Fields, frequency-bin encoding, continuous-variable quantum optics, VLF entanglement criteria, photonic circuit analysis.
