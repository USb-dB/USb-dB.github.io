At the IPCS Lab, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, I work on programmable silicon photonic circuits for switching and quantum-photonic applications. The device work brings together MMI couplers, directional couplers, and dual-mode MZI switches, with an emphasis on routing spatial modes through integrated waveguide networks.

I designed scalable Clements MZI meshes for dual-mode and higher-order spatial-mode switching, and completed GDS layout and post-layout analysis in KLayout. Current investigation explores how programmable Clements meshes can support Gaussian Boson Sampling (GBS), where phase control and circuit imperfections affect the output statistics of a photonic quantum processor.

Related work includes receiver-side MATLAB analysis of a 10 GHz OOK intensity-modulated, direct-detection optical link through an 8x8 thermo-optic MZI switch. The workflow applies gain control, normalization, adaptive thresholding, bit-error-rate computation, and eye-diagram analysis across routing configurations.

<strong>Tools and methods:</strong> MMI and directional couplers, dual-mode MZI switches, Clements meshes, GDS layout, KLayout, MATLAB, eye diagrams, BER analysis.
